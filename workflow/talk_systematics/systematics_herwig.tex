\begin{frame}{What Herwig changes}
\vspace{1em}
\begin{itemize}\itemsep1.2em

\item \key{Unfolding MC} --- response, purity, efficiency, truth
\item \key{Corrections} --- 2SV+UParT efficiency, EEC weight
\end{itemize}
\vspace{1.5em}
{\small Each one swapped to Herwig on its own, and the whole workflow swapped together.\\
The systematic is the difference to Pythia8.}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{frame}{Why not add them in quadrature}
\begin{columns}[c]
\column{0.44\textwidth}
\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{figs/syst_pythia.pdf}

\column{0.54\textwidth}
{\small
Quadrature assumes the sources are \key{independent}. These are one change ---
Pythia8 $\to$ Herwig --- seen in two places, so they are \key{fully correlated}.\\[1.0em]

Squaring also \key{discards the sign}: shifts that should partly cancel are added,
and shifts that should add are shortened to $\sqrt{a^2+b^2} < |a|+|b|$.\\[1.0em]

So it is wrong \key{in both directions} --- up to $11\times$ too large where the two
cancel, \num{$31\%$} too small where they add. Not a conservative choice.\\[1.0em]

Swapping the whole workflow needs \key{no assumption at all}: every correlation is
included by construction.
}
\end{columns}
\end{frame}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{frame}{Take home}
\vspace{0.8em}
{\small
\begin{itemize}\itemsep0.9em
\item The generator systematic is \key{one number per bin}: the difference between the
      analysis run entirely with Pythia8 and entirely with Herwig ---
      \num{$0.15\%$} to \num{$10.9\%$}.
\item It is \key{not} the quadrature of the individual swaps. Those are two views of the
      same change, and they add with sign --- quadrature is wrong in both directions.
\item The individual swaps are still worth having, as a \key{diagnostic}: template fit and
      unfolding MC contribute comparably, and often with opposite sign.
\item The b-tagging corrections differ by $3$--$4\%$ between generators but \key{cancel}
      in the normalised EEC --- only their shape matters.
\end{itemize}
